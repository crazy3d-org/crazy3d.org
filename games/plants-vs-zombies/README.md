# Plants Vs Zombies

Classic lane-based tower defense. Collect sunlight, place plants with distinct roles, and stop escalating zombie waves before they reach the house.

## How to play

- **Goal:** Build a lawn defense strong enough to clear each wave.
- **Select / place plant:** Mouse click or tap a lawn lane
- **Collect sunlight:** Click or tap
- **Remove a plant:** Shovel / in-game tool
- **Pause:** Esc or in-game button
