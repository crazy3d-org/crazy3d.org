# Strange Packages

Point-and-click horror puzzle escape. You wake in a dim room of cryptic packages, papers, and locked objects — inspect carefully, use inventory clues, and unlock the way out.

## How to play

- **Goal:** Investigate packages and clues, apply items in the right order, and escape the room.
- **Inspect / select:** Left click or tap
- **Move between views:** On-screen arrows or scene directions
- **Inventory:** Open from the bottom bar; select an item, then click its target
