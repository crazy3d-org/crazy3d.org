# Backrooms Escape

A tense maze escape in the yellow Backrooms. Read the map, complete local challenges, and keep distance from creatures that patrol the corridors — navigation and observation matter more than combat.

## How to play

- **Goal:** Explore the maze, finish objectives, avoid creatures, and reach the exit.
- **Move:** Keyboard movement keys shown in-game
- **Look / aim:** Mouse or on-screen look control
- **Select / interact:** Click or on-screen select
